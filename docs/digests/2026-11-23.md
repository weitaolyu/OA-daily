**摘要**

> Niobium thin films are a key component of superconducting detectors and electronics, and film surface quality, surface roughness, and geometrical dimensional accuracy affect many performance metrics. Atomic layer etching (ALE) is of interest for modifying film surfaces owing to its Angstrom-scale etching precision and surface smoothing characteristics. Here, we report a plasma-enhanced ALE process for Nb based on sequential O$\_2$ plasma and H$\_2$/SF$\_6$ plasma exposures. The process achieves an etch per cycle of 5-7 Angstrom/cycle and exhibits surface smoothing. The superconducting transition temperature of the ALE-treated films is maintained up to the expected decrease associated with reduced film thickness. Our ALE process could find applications in superconducting electronics for which surface quality and precise thickness control are of high importance.
