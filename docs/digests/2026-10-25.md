> We report the first detection of TiO in star-forming regions based on Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array observations of Orion Source I, a well-characterized massive protostar. Multiple rotational transitions are identified, with emission spatially resolved within ∼50 au, showing a compact distribution with a velocity structure consistent with the base of a rotating outflow. The spatial and velocity distributions of TiO are consistent with those of AlO, with both species being key dust-seeding refractory molecules. The column density of TiO is derived to be (3.0 ± 0.4) × 10 15 cm -2, corresponding to X TiO/SiO ∼ 12.8 ± 1.7 × 10 -3, higher than the CI chondrite X Ti/Si value and indicative of efficient dust-to-gas conversion near the protostar.
