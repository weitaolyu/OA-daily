We use this extraordinary geometry to study the impact of stellar ultraviolet (UV) irradiation on disk chemistry, temperature, and line formation. We present new ALMA observations of this disk, covering CO, 13 CO, C^18O, CN, HCN, HCO^+$, CS, and C\_3H\_2. Combined with previously published C\_2H, N\_2H^+ and H\_2CO observations, these data constitute an extensive dataset of fourteen ALMA lines from nine different molecules. We subdivide these data into two disk halves: the illuminated part and the shadowed part.
